# AI Discernment and Diligence

## Discernment
How to evaluate AI output, from coder standpoint.

- functional verification: does it work?
- production readiness: does it work with real inputs under load?
- intent verification: does it solve the user need?
- quality: is it good (and not 'just good enough') ?
- responsible: void of any bias, lack of invented facts

Taste and judgement, thinking outside the box.

## What quality experience is
- clarity: only show what's needed, no unnecessary distractors
- hierarchy: visual weight shows the importance of an item
- accessibility
- feedback: sane error messages, toasts, acknowledgements

## Iterating with AI
- no wishes, but specific instructions

## Diligence

How to assure yourself what the AI build is good.
- tests, tests. edge cases, error cases
- observability
- feature flags
